GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
before you go.

Clear answers for a clear start.

How do I add delivery stops?

Enter a customer name and address, then search for coordinates, select a point on the map, or enter latitude and longitude. You can add up to 20 delivery stops, plus your starting location. Demo search supports the sample New Delhi landmarks; other addresses need a configured geocoder.

How is the route calculated?

Python uses nearest-neighbor initialization and 2-opt improvement to suggest an efficient order. It compares the result with your original order and keeps the shorter option. This is a suggestion, not a guarantee of the mathematically shortest route.

Do I need an account?

No. You can plan, compare, and download routes as a guest. Register for a free local account when you want to save routes and reopen them from your dashboard.

Are the distances and travel times exact?

Road mode uses OSRM road data. Travel times are estimates without live traffic or delivery time. Demo mode uses straight-line distances and assumes 25 km/h. Demo results are unsuitable for navigation.

What happens if road routing is unavailable?

Your entries stay in place and the planner shows an error. You can retry or explicitly switch to the straight-line demo. Approximate results are never silently substituted for road routes.
